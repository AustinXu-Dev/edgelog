# Edgelog

**The trading journal built for traders who take their edge seriously.**

Edgelog is a personal trading performance platform that helps you track every trade, reflect on your process, and uncover what's actually working — backed by real data and AI-powered coaching.

---

## Why Edgelog

Most traders lose their edge not because of bad setups, but because of patterns they can't see — overtrading on Fridays, revenge trading after drawdowns, ignoring their best-performing playbook. Edgelog puts those patterns in front of you.

---

## What's Inside

### Trade Journal
Log every trade with full detail — instrument, direction, entry/exit, P&L, R-multiple, screenshots, and personal notes. Every trade tells a story; make sure you're reading it.

### Playbooks
Define your named strategies and tag them to each trade. See exactly which playbooks are printing and which ones are costing you — no more guessing.

### Daily Journal
Reflect on each session with mood tracking and written entries. Link the specific trades that defined your day. Build the habit of reviewing your process, not just your P&L.

### Analytics Dashboard
Equity curve, P&L by instrument, win rate by day of week, performance by time of day. A heatmap calendar showing your best and worst months at a glance.

### AI Coach
On-demand AI analysis powered by Groq. Get a deep breakdown of each trading session or a full behavioral pattern report across your last 200 trades — strategy win rates, timing blind spots, and a single prioritized recommendation.

### Multi-Account Support
Manage live, funded, and sim accounts separately. Switch instantly and keep performance data cleanly isolated.

### CSV Import & Export
Already trading? Import your history in bulk. Export any filtered slice of your trades anytime.
